# UC026 — Kiểm tra ràng buộc kế hoạch cập bến

**Status:** Implemented (2026-11-19). Candidate for Completed once the owner accepts the assumptions (A-1..A-3, A-6): AC-2 is enforced on save, submission and approval of plans made by UC-028/029/030 (built), and AC-1 and AC-3 passed. The berth plan chart marks violations from the server result (`docs/testing/a3-chart-evidence.md`). Evidence: `docs/testing/uc024-uc026-evidence.md`. · **Module:** M2 · **Priority:** H · **Rollout:** Đợt 1
Source: `usecases.txt` lines 2619–2659. FR-BRT-04; NFR-ACC-02, NFR-PER-02. Rules BR-01, BR-02, BR-03 in the specification.

## 1. Objective
Check a proposed berth placement against the technical and business rules, and report each rule as pass or violation with a reason.

## 2. Actors
- **Primary:** Hệ thống.
- **Supporting:** Điều phối viên (requests the check and fixes violations).

## 3. Permissions
- `DIEU_PHOI_VIEN`, `BAN_QUAN_LY`.

## 4. Preconditions
- The voyage is in the queue (UC-020) or has a draft plan (UC-028).
- The berth exists (UC-024).

## 5. Postconditions
- **Success:** a result per rule (`passed`, or the violation and its reason). Nothing is stored by the check itself.
- **Failure (missing data):** no result; the plan cannot be saved (spec 6a).

## 6. Main flow
1. The check receives the vessel and the placement: berth, position start and end, planned start and end, required cranes.
2. LOA ≤ berth length; draft ≤ berth depth.
3. Berth type matches the cargo class; enough cranes.
4. No overlap with another vessel in the same berth stretch and time (BR-01); clearance of at least the buffer (BR-02, default 15 m); dangerous goods only at an allowed berth (BR-03).
5. Berth is not in maintenance during the placement.
6. The result lists each rule with pass or violation.

## 7. Alternative flows
- **4a. Violation found:** the placement is marked on the plan chart for the coordinator to change (UC-028).

## 8. Exception flows
- **6a. Missing data (for example vessel without LOA):** "thiếu dữ liệu", no result, plan cannot be saved.

## 9. Business rules
- **BR-01** Two vessels do not occupy the same berth stretch at the same time.
- **BR-02** Adjacent vessels keep a safety buffer. Default 15 m, from `system_parameters` (UC-007).
- **BR-03** Dangerous goods only at berths that allow them (`allows_dangerous`).
- **BR-04** Vessel LOA and draft limits come from the vessel profile and berth catalog.
- **BR-05** A maintenance interval blocks placements that overlap it (UC-034 creates intervals; the check reads them).

## 10. Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 Every violation names the rule and the reason. **Passed** (unit and integration for all eight rules; e2e shows the rule, its outcome and the reason).
- AC-2 100% of saved plans have no violation unless overridden with a reason. **Passed for the manual path** (UC-028/029: every save and submission repeats the check in the berth lock; database `CK_bp_reviewed`). Plans from AI proposals (UC-027) are not built.
- AC-3 The check uses the buffer from parameters, not a constant. **Passed** (integration: changing `BERTH_SAFETY_BUFFER_M` to 30 changes the result; removing it gives 503 `PARAMETER_UNAVAILABLE`, no silent default).

## 11. Assumptions and open items
| ID | Item | Rationale | Affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-1 | Position is a metre offset along the quay; a vessel occupies `[position_start_m, position_end_m]`. Buffer is added on each side of a neighbour. | Specification speaks of "đoạn bến" and "khoảng đệm" without a formula. | BR-01, BR-02 |
| A-2 | Overlap is checked in a transaction that locks the berth row, so two concurrent plans cannot both pass. | A single CHECK cannot express overlap; a trigger is avoided. | Section 6 |
| A-3 | The cranes requirement is a count of available cranes on the berth, compared with the plan's required cranes. | Specification says "đủ số cẩu bờ". | Section 4 |
| A-4 | **Replaced (2026-11-19, decision of the project owner).** Reefer cargo is not treated as hazardous, and a value sent by the client is not evidence. BR-03 uses the voyage's **verified cargo hazard** (`NONE` or `DANGEROUS`, set by a coordinator with a basis through `PUT /voyages/:id/cargo-hazard`, audited). A berth with the dangerous-goods label needs no fact. At any other berth, an unverified voyage gives MISSING_DATA: no plan can be saved until the cargo is verified. The request field `dangerous_goods` was removed. Who verifies, and whether the agent's declaration should become a notice field, is open. | Specification BR-03 needs a fact the notice does not carry. | BR-03 |
| A-5 | Three outcomes per rule: PASS, VIOLATION, MISSING_DATA. Overall: **MISSING_DATA if any rule lacks data, else VIOLATION if any fails, else PASS** (changed from "violation wins": a stored VIOLATION now always means every rule could be evaluated, which the plan table relies on for overrides). `passed` = PASS only. `overridable` is true only for a VIOLATION whose failed rules all allow an exception. | Spec 6a; decision F-1. | Section 8 |
| A-6 | Plans that count as neighbors: PENDING_APPROVAL, APPROVED and COMPLETED, on the same berth, sharing time; the voyage's own plans are ignored; drafts do not reserve. A gap exactly equal to the buffer passes; a stretch ending exactly at the berth length passes; touching times do not overlap. | The specification gives no boundary rules. | BR-01, BR-02 |
| A-7 | The voyage must be WAITING_BERTH (in the queue). **New rule `BERTH_IN_SERVICE` (2026-11-19, decision of the project owner):** an OUT_OF_SERVICE berth is a violation that **cannot be overridden** (no exception mechanism is documented); a deactivated berth (`is_active = 0`) is refused with 400. | New plans must not go to inactive berths. | Section 4 |
| A-8 | The check is a read: `POST /berth-plan-checks`, any role with M2 view, no audit row. | Nothing is changed or stored (postcondition). | Section 5 |

## 12. Demo scenario
1. Check a 250 m vessel at a 300 m berth. Expect pass.
2. Check a 320 m vessel at the same berth. Expect LOA violation with reason.
3. Check a second vessel overlapping the first. Expect overlap violation.

## Update 2026-11-19: occupancy of a berthed vessel (A-6 refined)
The neighbour query now uses the shared occupancy definition (`repositories/occupancy.js`, also used by UC-027): a plan occupies from the earlier of its planned start and the ATA; a completed plan ends at the ATD; a BERTHED vessel whose planned end has passed and which has not departed occupies its stretch with no known end. Before this change such a vessel stopped blocking its stretch at its planned end, contrary to UC-022 A-2 and UC-025 A-5. The decision rule is unchanged; only the data fed to BR-01 and BR-02 is corrected.

## Update 2026-11-19: violations on the chart (4a)
The berth plan chart marks a stored plan whose UC-026 result failed (red outline and "!") or that carries an override ("⚠"), and shows the server's result for a dragged placement (ghost block with the failed rules and reasons). The chart judges nothing: every result comes from the server check. Evidence: `docs/testing/a3-chart-evidence.md`.
