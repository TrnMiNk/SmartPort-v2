## 1. Objective
Show vessel schedules as a list and as a Gantt chart by day or week, filtered by status, agent and berth.

## 2. Actors
- **Primary:** Điều phối viên.
- **Supporting:** Ban quản lý (views).

## 3. Permissions
- View: `DIEU_PHOI_VIEN`, `BAN_QUAN_LY`, `QUAN_TRI_HE_THONG`.

## 4. Preconditions
- The user is logged in.

## 5. Postconditions
- **Success:** the schedule matches the filters and the time window.
- **Failure:** an error with a retry option; no partial data shown as complete.

## 6. Main flow
1. The user opens "Lịch tàu" (`GET /api/v1/voyages/schedule?view=list`).
2. The list shows voyages with status, agent, berth (if planned), ETA, ATA, ATD.
3. The user switches to Gantt and picks a day or week view (`view=gantt`).
4. The user filters by status, agent or berth.
5. The Gantt draws each voyage in its berth lane between its planned or actual times.
6. The user opens a voyage to see its lifecycle.

## 7. Alternative flows
- **4a. No voyage matches:** "Không có dữ liệu".

## 8. Exception flows
- **5a. Load error:** message and retry.

## 9. Business rules
- **BR-1** Lifecycle order: `ANNOUNCED → VALIDATED → WAITING_BERTH → BERTH_ASSIGNED → BERTHED → DEPARTED` (this delivery; `BERTH_ASSIGNED` added by decision C-1, set when the berth plan is approved). `MISSING_DOCUMENTS` and `REJECTED` are terminal branches of validation.
- **BR-2** Times are shown in UTC+7; stored in UTC.
- **BR-3** A voyage's berth comes from its approved plan (UC-030) once one exists.

## 10. Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 Filtering by berth or agent returns the matching rows only. **Passed** (integration "AC-1", e2e "UC-021").
- AC-2 The Gantt bar matches the stored times. **Passed** (integration "AC-2" and "window"; e2e checks the bar at 33.33% and 50% of the day, and the title shows 08:00 and 20:00 UTC+7).

## 11. Assumptions and open items
| ID | Item | Rationale | Affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-1 | The Gantt uses planned times for planned voyages and ATA or ATD for actual ones. | Specification says "thời điểm các chuyến tàu" without saying which. | Section 6 |
| A-2 | Day or week windows are fixed to the operator's calendar (UTC+7). | Not specified; reasonable default. | BR-2 |
| A-3 | A week starts on Monday. | Common in Vietnam; not specified. | Step 3 |
| A-4 | A voyage is in the window when its interval overlaps it (start before window end, end after window start; touching edges are outside). A voyage with no interval is placed by its declared ETA. | Needed so a Gantt answer is complete and has no duplicates. | Step 5 |
| A-5 | The bar runs from ATA (else planned start) to ATD (else planned end). A berthed voyage without ATD keeps the planned end. | A-1 applied to partial actuals. | Step 5 |
| A-6 | Only an APPROVED or COMPLETED berth plan gives a berth (BR-3). A pending plan does not. | Spec: "kế hoạch được duyệt". | Step 2 |
| A-7 | The list ignores the day/week window and is paged (10 per page, oldest first); the Gantt is limited to 31 days and 500 voyages and says so when cut. | Keeps answers small and never "partial but complete-looking". | Steps 2, 3 |
| A-8 | Permission is `view` on M1_VESSELS (coordinator, management, administrator, warehouse staff per the SRS matrix). Agents cannot open the schedule. | Spec lists the coordinator and management; the matrix grants view to these roles. | Section 3 |

## 12. Demo scenario
1. Show the list for the next seven days. Expect all planned voyages.
2. Switch to Gantt, filter by one berth. Expect only that berth's lane.
