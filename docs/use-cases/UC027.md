## 1. Objective
Ask the AI service for one to three ranked berth placements for up to 20 queued vessels, store the proposal, and show it. If the AI is unavailable, the coordinator places vessels manually.

## 2. Actors
- **Primary:** Điều phối viên.
- **Supporting:** Dịch vụ AI (berth recommendation, external).

## 3. Permissions
- `DIEU_PHOI_VIEN`. The AI call is made on the server; the browser never calls the provider.

## 4. Preconditions
- Vessels are in the queue (UC-020). The berth catalog exists (UC-024).
- Placement checks (UC-026) run for each vessel before the proposal is shown (main flow step 2).

## 5. Postconditions
- **Success (`AVAILABLE`):** one to three ranked proposals shown and stored with the AI request and response.
- **Failure (`UNAVAILABLE`):** no proposal; manual placement offered (UC-028).

## 6. Main flow
1. The coordinator opens "Kế hoạch cập bến" and selects vessels (at most 20).
2. The API runs the placement check for each vessel (UC-026).
3. The API sends the request to the berth service (queue and berth states).
4. The service returns 1 to 3 ranked placements: berth, position, planned start and end, expected wait, score.
5. The API stores the request, the response and each proposal, with model version.
6. The screen shows the proposals and the plan chart.

## 7. Alternative flows
- **4a. Only one feasible placement:** shown with a note.

## 8. Exception flows
- **3a. No response within `AI_TIMEOUT_MS` (10 s), error, or no feasible placement:** message shown; the coordinator places the vessels manually (UC-028). Stored as `UNAVAILABLE` with the reason.
- **More than 20 vessels selected:** refused with `VALIDATION_FAILED` before any call.

## 9. Business rules
- **BR-1** Proposals respect the placement rules of UC-026.
- **BR-2** The AI request, response and each proposal are stored (FR-BRT-12). Proposals are never deleted.
- **BR-3** A proposal is not a plan. Acceptance is UC-028.
- **BR-4** The AI result is never simulated. Without a provider the result is `UNAVAILABLE`.

## 10. Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 A response within 10 seconds for up to 20 queued vessels. **Passed for the local optimizer** (20 queued voyages, 8 berths: about 0.4 to 0.6 s end to end on SQL Server; fixture data). **Blocked for an external AI provider** (none exists, D3).
- AC-2 Every proposal is stored for quality review. **Passed** (migration 0006): request snapshot with hash, the full answer, each vessel outcome (also "unschedulable" with reasons), each proposal with its UC-026 result and explanation, and the human decision (FR-BRT-12: ACCEPTED, MODIFIED, IGNORED).
- AC-3 The manual path works. **Passed**: UC-028 is unchanged; a vessel the engine cannot place, or a recommendation that is ignored, is planned by hand (integration and browser).

## 16. Local optimization slice (2026-11-19, approved direction)

### 16.1 What was built and what it is
A **local, deterministic berth optimizer** (policy `UC027-OPT-V1`, `src/policy/berthOptimizer.js`) used as decision support. It is not machine learning, makes no prediction, and has no confidence value: its `score` is a utility `1 / (1 + cost)`, shown as such. UC-026 is the authority: every candidate is checked by `placementService.evaluateForPlan` and only a PASS is shown; a failed candidate is counted and dropped. Missing information is reported, never treated as a pass. Manual planning (UC-028) is unchanged, and a recommendation never creates or approves a plan.

### 16.3 Algorithm and provisional policy parameters
Vessels are handled in queue order (UC-020). For each in-service berth that fits (type, LOA, draft, hazard, cranes) the engine finds the **earliest** slot at or after max(effective ETA, now) rounded up to the grid, at the **lowest position** where the vessel keeps the safety buffer from other reservations and avoids maintenance (event-point search over reservation ends). Candidates are ranked by `cost = wait_h + 0.25 * duration_h + 0.1 * unused_length + 0.05 * unused_cranes + 0.1 * ordinary_cargo_on_dangerous_berth`; ties: earlier start, lower berth id, lower position; at most 3 verified candidates kept. The rank-1 placement of each vessel is held as a tentative reservation for the vessels after it, so the rank-1 placements of one run do not collide. Reservations counted: pending, approved and completed plans (as UC-026 does for a draft).

### 16.4 Human decisions (FR-BRT-12)
A draft made from a proposal (`proposal_id` in `POST /berth-plans`) is checked again by UC-026, gets `source = AI` when it equals the proposal and `AI_ADJUSTED` when it differs, and records **ACCEPTED** or **MODIFIED** in `berth_plan_decisions`; editing the draft re-classifies it. **IGNORED** is recorded by `POST /berth-recommendations/:id/ignore`. UC-026 runs again at save, submission and approval (UC-028 to UC-030, unchanged). Limit: a coordinator who plans by hand without opening a recommendation leaves no decision row (the plan is `MANUAL`).

### 16.5 Quality evidence (superseded by section 17.4; the first comparison used one fixture)
On 30 seeded, **constructed** scenarios (20 vessels, 7 berths) the policy was compared with a simulated first-fit coordinator: mean waiting 4.65 h against 4.39 h (the baseline is lower), maximum 28.07 h against 27.17 h, unused berth length 0.38 against 0.40, unused cranes 0.15 against 0.17, same number of vessels placed. **No improvement in waiting time was found and none is claimed**; the fit terms are marginally better. The baseline models a coordinator, it is not real manual data, and no real port data exists.

### 16.6 Demo scenario (local optimizer)
1. Coordinator, queue panel, "Đề xuất bến": ranked proposals per vessel with reasons, "UC-026 đạt", and a banner saying it is a local optimizer, not machine learning, and that the AI forecast is not available.
2. "Dùng đề xuất" opens the normal draft form prefilled; save as is (AI, ACCEPTED) or change something (AI_ADJUSTED, MODIFIED).
3. "Bỏ qua đề xuất" records IGNORED; plan by hand.
4. Submit; the manager approves; nothing is approved by the recommendation.
5. A vessel with no feasible berth (for example LOA longer than every berth) shows "Không xếp được tự động" with the reasons.

## 17. Quality hardening (2026-11-19)
Evidence: `docs/testing/uc027-hardening-evidence.md`.

### 17.1 Occupancy defect (confirmed, fixed)
**Confirmed business rules:** UC-022 A-2 and UC-025 A-5: a vessel that is BERTHED keeps its berth in use until it actually departs, whatever its planned end. **Defect:** UC-026 and the recommendation both read only the planned interval, so a BERTHED vessel whose planned departure had passed (no departure recorded) no longer blocked its stretch: a recommendation, a UC-026 check and a draft all treated the stretch as free (reproduced by a failing integration test first). **Fix:** one shared definition, `repositories/occupancy.js`, used by the UC-026 neighbour query and by the recommendation snapshot: a plan occupies from the earlier of its planned start and the ATA (early berthing); a completed plan ends at the ATD; a BERTHED vessel past its planned end with no ATD occupies with **no known end** (open-ended). Effects: UC-026 now reports OVERLAP against such a vessel at save, submission and approval, and the engine never proposes that stretch; after the departure the stretch is free again from the actual time. Regression tests: integration (planned departure in the past, no departure; before planned departure; after departure) and unit (open-ended reservation).

### 17.2 Cargo hazard consistency (P-7 against UC-026)
Reviewed at recommendation, draft save, submission and approval with one integration test (labelled and ordinary berths, unverified, verified safe and dangerous cargo, a hazard changed after a proposal). **Result: no contradiction between the engine and UC-026.** Unverified cargo is never treated as safe (an ordinary berth gives MISSING_DATA at save, which cannot be overridden); dangerous cargo only at a labelled berth (an override can let a draft be saved and submitted but approval refuses it); a labelled berth needs no fact (UC-026 BR-03 as decided); a change of the verified hazard is caught at approval and by the next recommendation. Documented inconsistencies (no new hazard rule was invented): (a) P-7 treats declared TEU 0 as missing, but UC-013 and the schema accept 0; kept as provisional because the handling time cannot be estimated without TEU, to be decided by the owner; (b) the preference that keeps labelled berths free for dangerous cargo (weight 0.1) is a provisional policy, not a UC-026 rule; (c) question for the owner: should unverified cargo be allowed at a labelled berth at all (UC-026 allows it).

### 17.3 Provenance (migration 0007)
Plans made from a local proposal were stored with source `AI` / `AI_ADJUSTED`, which labelled an algorithm as AI. Now: `MANUAL`; `LOCAL_OPTIMIZER` (equals a proposal) and `LOCAL_OPTIMIZER_ADJUSTED` (changed); `AI` and `AI_ADJUSTED` are reserved for an actual external AI provider (none exists). Request origin `engine_kind`: `LOCAL_OPTIMIZER` or `EXTERNAL_AI` (the historical `EXTERNAL_PROVIDER` stays valid and is shown as EXTERNAL_AI). The plan list and review dialog show "Thuật toán tối ưu cục bộ" or "Dịch vụ AI ngoài"; the audit row of a request carries `engine_kind` and `policy_version`. Compatibility: no old migration was edited; existing `AI` values are unchanged unless the plan came from a LOCAL_OPTIMIZER request (relabelled by 0007 with one audit row); API consumers reading `source` see the new values for local proposals (only possible since 0006, not deployed). Rollback refuses while a LOCAL_OPTIMIZER plan or an EXTERNAL_AI request exists.

### 17.4 Algorithm V2 and quality measurements
Policy `UC027-OPT-V2` (V1 stays available: a stored snapshot replays with the version it names). Diagnosis: V1 ranks each vessel's candidates by cost and takes the best one for that vessel alone. Choosing a tight berth for one vessel can block the ones behind it, so V1 helped in light traffic and was no better than first-fit in congested traffic (the first fixture, 4.65 h against 4.39 h, was one unlucky sample). V2 keeps the queue order and gives **every vessel its earliest feasible start**, and uses a bounded beam search (width 8, 4 alternatives per step, work cap 2000 searches, a counter and not a clock) only to choose WHICH berth and position, looking at the vessels behind. A variant that let earlier vessels wait to help later ones was measured and **rejected** (it sacrifices queue priority). Results on 4 load profiles x 40 seeded constructed scenarios (identical snapshots; first-fit models a coordinator and is not real data): mean waiting V2 against first-fit: moderate 3.49 against 4.27 h, congested 12.62 against 13.25 h, light 1.04 against 1.45 h, few berths 23.45 against 23.82 h; per-seed paired result (better/equal/worse) 28/6/6, 30/6/4, 24/15/1, 11/29/0; unscheduled vessels equal; worst single wait equal; slowest engine run 8 ms. V1 against first-fit was mixed (22/2/16, 14/7/19, 22/16/2, 8/23/9). **These are constructed scenarios: they support "V2 is not worse than first-fit and usually better on invented queues", not an improvement at a real port, and not any AI claim.**

### 17.5 Assumptions: confirmed versus provisional
Confirmed (decided before): UC-026 rules and their parameters, the buffer parameter, occupancy until actual departure (UC-022/UC-025), hazard from the verified voyage fact, 1 to 3 proposals, 20 vessels, 10 s. **Still provisional and unapproved: P-1 to P-7, P-9, and the new P-10 (beam width 8, 4 alternatives, work cap 2000; chosen from measurements).** P-6 is unchanged. P-8 is replaced by the rule in 17.1.
