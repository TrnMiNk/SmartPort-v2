# UC028 — Chấp nhận hoặc điều chỉnh phương án cập bến

**Status:** Implemented (2026-11-19). Not Completed: assumptions (override permission, CH-1..CH-3) are unapproved and AI-sourced plans cannot exist until a real AI provider does; plans from the local optimizer (`LOCAL_OPTIMIZER`, `LOCAL_OPTIMIZER_ADJUSTED`) and manual plans work. The plan chart with drag and drop (FR-BRT-03, steps 1, 2, 4, 2a) is built: a drop opens the existing check/save dialog; every save is a server check (UC-026) with revision checks and an audited override. Evidence: `docs/testing/uc028-uc029-evidence.md`, `docs/testing/a3-chart-evidence.md`. · **Module:** M2 · **Priority:** H · **Rollout:** Đợt 1
Source: `usecases.txt` lines 2702–2740. FR-BRT-03, FR-BRT-06; NFR-ACC-02, NFR-SEC-04, NFR-USA-01.

## 1. Objective
Let the coordinator accept a proposal, reject all proposals, or adjust a placement by moving it on the chart. Violations need an explicit override with a reason.

## 2. Actors
- **Primary:** Điều phối viên.

## 3. Permissions
- `DIEU_PHOI_VIEN`.

## 4. Preconditions
- A proposal exists (UC-027), or the coordinator places vessels manually.

## 5. Postconditions
- **Success:** a draft plan (`DRAFT`) with the final placement, the source (`AI`, `AI_ADJUSTED` or `MANUAL`), any override reason, and the placement check result stored.
- **Failure:** no plan created.

## 6. Main flow
1. The screen shows the plan chart: time on one axis, quay position on the other.
2. The coordinator accepts a proposal (`POST /api/v1/berth-plans` with `source = AI`), or drags a vessel to adjust it (`source = AI_ADJUSTED`).
3. The API runs the placement check (UC-026) on the chosen placement.
4. Each violation is marked on the chart.
5. The coordinator confirms the final placement.

## 7. Alternative flows
- **2a. Reject all proposals:** manual placement by dragging (`source = MANUAL`), then step 3.
- **3a. Violation after adjustment:** warning. The coordinator may continue only with an override reason; the reason is audited.

## 8. Exception flows
- **5a. Concurrent change to another plan:** reload and choose again (`409 CONFLICT`).

## 9. Business rules
- **BR-1** Violations are always shown and always warned (spec).
- **BR-2** An override requires a reason of 1 to 500 characters (`CK_bp_override`).
- **BR-3** The source is recorded; AI proposals keep their link (`proposal_id`, `CK_bp_source_proposal`).
- **BR-4** Rules BR-01, BR-02, BR-03 are checked again at the final step (UC-029).

## 10. Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 An adjustment that violates a rule always produces a warning. **Passed** (integration and e2e: a violating save or edit is refused with every failed rule until an override reason is given).
- AC-2 Every override has a reason and an audit row. **Passed** (integration: `BERTH_PLAN_OVERRIDE` audit row with reason and rules; database: `CK_bp_override_set`; e2e).
- AC-3 A plan with an override has `source` and reason saved. **Passed** for `source = MANUAL` (integration, e2e, migration test). `AI` and `AI_ADJUSTED` are refused (400) until UC-027 exists.

## 11. Assumptions and open items
| ID | Item | Rationale | Affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-1 | "Chấp nhận" creates a `DRAFT` plan, not an approved one. Submission is UC-029. | Specification keeps approval separate. | Section 5 |
| A-2 | Drag and drop is a frontend interaction; each drop is a server check, so the server result is authoritative. | Frontend rule: business rules stay in the API. | Section 6 |
| A-3 | The legacy FE test name "accept" maps to creating a draft from a proposal (legacy G6). The contract names the operation explicitly. | Legacy evidence. | Section 6 |
| A-4 | One draft per voyage (database: `UX_bp_draft_per_voyage`). A voyage with a pending or approved plan gets no new draft. A draft reserves nothing: only pending, approved and completed plans count as neighbors in the check. | A coordinator edits the draft instead of piling up copies; the specification does not say. | Section 5 |
| A-5 | A draft can be saved with a passing check, or with violations plus an override reason. It cannot be saved with missing data (UC-026 6a), whatever the reason. The out-of-service berth rule cannot be overridden (no exception mechanism is documented). | UC-026 6a; decision "inactive berths". | 3a |
| A-6 | The right to override is the permission `override` on M2_BERTHS, derived from this use case and granted to the coordinator only (it is not a column of the SRS matrix). Which violations may be overridden is **open**: today all except the out-of-service berth, including overlap (BR-01) and dangerous goods (BR-03). | Spec 3a lets the coordinator override constraints without naming exceptions. Needs a business decision. | 3a |
| A-7 | Editing a draft re-runs the check and needs the current revision (`If-Match`); a plan keeps its voyage. No discard action exists yet. | Spec 5a; smallest workflow. | 5a |
| A-8 | The override is stored beside the check result, never inside `passed`: `passed` stays false when a rule failed (decision F-1). | Keeps the meaning of the evaluation. | BR-2 |

## Update 2026-11-19: plan chart and drag and drop (FR-BRT-03; UC-028 steps 1, 2, 4, 2a)
The chart (one berth at a time; 1, 3 or 7 days; time along x, position along the berth on y) shows approved, pending, draft, completed and actual occupancy plus maintenance from `GET /berths/:id/chart`. A draft or a proposal can be dragged; a queued vessel without a plan is dragged from a tray (stretch = LOA, default stay 12 h as a UI default, assumption CH-3). Dropping does **not** save: it asks the server to check the placement (UC-026), draws the result on the chart (pass, violation with the rule reasons, or missing data) and opens the existing dialog with the dropped values, where the coordinator saves, overrides with a reason (3a, audited) or cancels. Revision checks (5a, concurrent edit) and permissions are the existing ones. A keyboard user opens the same dialog with Enter on a block. Evidence: `docs/testing/a3-chart-evidence.md`. Status unchanged (Implemented).
